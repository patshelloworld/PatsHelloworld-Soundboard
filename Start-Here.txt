PATSHELLOWORLD SOUNDBOARD

1. Unzip the download and move PatsHelloworld-Soundboard.app to
   Applications. Open it on your Mac. If asked about the developer, see
   https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445 for macOS launch options.
2. Put your iPhone and Mac on the same Wi-Fi. If macOS asks for local
   network access, allow it so the iPhone can reach the app.
3. Scan the QR code with the iPhone camera and open the page in Safari.
   Tap a pad. The Mac plays the sound through its selected audio output.
4. In Safari, use Share > Add to Home Screen for quick access. Keep the
   Mac app open while you use the soundboard. You can minimize the window.

LOAD YOUR SOUNDS FOLDER
The app comes with 15 sounds. Place a folder named sounds next to
PatsHelloworld-Soundboard.app to load audio files from that folder.
Bundled sounds fill any remaining pads. The board adds pads as needed
when the folder has more audio files. Relaunch the Mac app after
changing the folder contents.
Sounds you assign with Customize take priority on those pads.
For an existing install, quit the app and replace only the .app file
with the updated one. Keep your existing sounds folder in place.

CUSTOMIZE A BUTTON
Select a pad on the Mac and click Customize. You can choose a WAV, MP3,
or other audio file, change the button name and icon, and set its volume.
Use Preview to hear your changes on the Mac before you save. The iPhone
updates automatically within a few seconds. Restore Default resets that
button to its sounds-folder or bundled sound and original settings.

CAPTURE SOUNDBOARD AUDIO IN OBS
In OBS Studio 30 or later, add a macOS Audio Capture source and choose
PatsHelloworld Soundboard as the application. Make a short local recording
while you tap a pad and play it back to check the level. If you already
capture all desktop audio, check that the sound enters the mix only once.

CONTROL OBS FROM THE IPHONE
1. Open OBS Studio 28 or later. In Tools > WebSocket Server Settings,
   enable the server and keep authentication on. Copy its port and
   password (the default port is 4455).
2. In the Mac soundboard, click OBS Settings and enter those details.
   To mute a custom microphone source, enter its exact name from the
   OBS Audio Mixer. Leave it blank to use the first enabled Mic/Aux.
3. Open the paired phone page and tap OBS. Tap a scene to take it
   directly to Program, or use the mic and recording buttons.
   Starting or stopping a recording asks for confirmation.

The OBS password stays on the Mac. Keep your phone pairing link private
because it can control OBS on your local network.

IF THE PHONE CANNOT CONNECT
Try Open on Mac to check the board locally. Check that both devices are
on the same Wi-Fi and allow local network access for the app if prompted.
If the Mac link works locally but the iPhone cannot resolve the .local
name, replace the hostname in the pairing URL with the Mac's Wi-Fi IP
address. Keep the port and the full path. Guest Wi-Fi may block connections
between devices.

The release download supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running
macOS 13 or later. The app is ad hoc signed and has no Apple notarization.
Source and build instructions are available on GitHub at
https://github.com/patshelloworld/PatsHelloworld-Soundboard
